# NextGen For Earth — Short Founder Demo Pitch

## 90-second opening

NextGen For Earth already has the mission, programmes, research activity and public presence. The next step is to connect those assets to the systems that coordinate delivery and demonstrate results.

We are building one organisational platform around that mission. The public website communicates the organisation's work and will include a dedicated Climate Intelligence hub for Kenya and East Africa. The hub will publish carefully reviewed climate indicators, regional trends and research insights with their sources, periods, methods and limitations visible. The hub distinguishes when a release was published, when its source was retrieved and how far the underlying data period extends; these timestamps are not a live-weather claim. The workspace is where authorised teams manage people, programmes, learning, research, climate information, partners and evidence.

The research layer is designed to take a publication or study from discovery and source attribution through proposal, protocol and ethics review, datasets, climate evidence, analysis and publication. Climate intelligence is not limited to manual field submissions: the architecture brings together external sources such as NASA POWER and CHIRPS, with Copernicus ERA5 subject to deployment configuration, alongside reviewed field observations.

The learning layer now demonstrates four pilot tracks, eight modules and 26 substantive lessons with further reading, formative quizzes, enrolment, assessment, mentorship and learner progress. The content is a subject-matter review pilot, not accredited or finally approved training.

The objective is practical: reduce fragmented records, make programme delivery easier to manage, give leadership a current operational view, and give partners and funders a clearer evidence trail.

## 7-minute demo sequence

1. **Public story (45 sec):** show the mission, programme pillars, research and published impact claims. Identify website-reported metrics as source claims, not audited results.
2. **Founder workspace (60 sec):** show organisation overview, people/roles, partner verification and audit controls.
3. **Research library (90 sec):** open a website-imported publication, show its source provenance, preview external scholarly metadata and create a new study in concept status.
4. **Research governance (60 sec):** show proposal/protocol/ethics gates, dataset submission/review and analysis provenance. Do not imply a concept is approved or active.
5. **Climate intelligence (75 sec):** open the populated public Climate Intelligence hub. Inspect the published synthetic rainfall and temperature series, regional comparison values, source register, methodology and linked research field note. State clearly that the series are deterministic staging fixtures—not observations, forecasts, provider data or scientific findings. Open the authenticated Climate Studio and demonstrate the separate real-provider workflow: bounded CHIRPS ingestion, sample inspection, independent batch quality review, release drafting and approval/publication queue. Never conflate the synthetic public showcase with live provider acceptance.
6. **Field-data loop (45 sec):** demonstrate the synthetic ODK simulator only when no live Central test endpoint is configured. Label all fixture records Synthetic demo and show duplicate suppression and pending review.
7. **Learning and programme delivery (60 sec):** show the four-track pilot catalogue, course structure, 26 substantive lessons, further-reading links, formative quizzes, enrolment/assessment workflow and programme activities. Explain that the content is ready for subject-matter review but is not accredited training.
8. **Close (25 sec):** return to the leadership proposition: one connected system for research, youth development, climate stewardship, healthier communities and evidence.

## Closing statement

We are not proposing another disconnected website or spreadsheet. We are building the operating layer that connects the organisation's people, programmes, research, learning, partners and evidence—so that its mission can be delivered, governed and measured with greater clarity.

## Demo integrity checklist

- Use dedicated staging/demo accounts and synthetic learner identities.
- Label imported website records, external provider data and synthetic fixtures.
- Public climate visuals must use published releases only; a verified observation is not automatically public.
- The staging synthetic climate showcase must carry its visible disclaimer on the catalogue and each detail page. Do not present it as live climate evidence.
- Before claiming live provider-backed climate intelligence, verify a successful provider ingestion, independent review and approved published release.
- Do not describe ODK simulator activity as a live Central connection.
- Do not present website-reported impact figures as independently audited.
- Do not present unverified partner prospects as signed partnerships.
- Do not present draft curriculum content as approved or accredited.
- Do not expose private records, credentials, storage keys or invitation links.


## Research-linked climate evidence

The public climate hub now includes a research-context layer. A CMS climate insight must reference both a published climate release and a separately published research publication. In staging, the seeded field note explicitly identifies its synthetic release and states that the linked SENIORS publication is independent context, not validation or analysis of the fixture values. This demonstrates the evidence-navigation experience without implying causation or empirical findings.


## Learning capacity

The learning workspace now includes four pilot tracks across research methods, climate and health, youth climate stewardship and Indigenous/community knowledge. Twenty-six substantive lessons include applied practice, self-checks and official further reading, supported by four formative quizzes and learner progress. These materials are positioned as a subject-matter review pilot rather than accredited training.
